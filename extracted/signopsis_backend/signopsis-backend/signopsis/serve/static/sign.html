The simulator turns the text into ISL signing with the 2D avatar, converts it to MediaPipe-style landmarks and streams them over the same WebSocket a real camera uses. The dashed chips are special cases. ambiguous uses two near-identical signs, so it triggers a "which one?" question. The unlisted chips are signs the recognizer doesn't know, so you can try the teach flow.

A name sign, a regional variant or work jargon: sign it 4 times, lowering your hands between samples. No retraining is needed.
